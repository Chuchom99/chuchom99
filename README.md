### Featured work

**[Blocklo](https://github.com/Chuchom99/blocklo)**: WhatsApp banking on 9PSB wallets
Transfers, airtime, data and bill payments through a WhatsApp bot and a REST API. Every payment goes through one path (draft, PIN authorization, single execution, then automatic reconciliation). PINs are entered in encrypted WhatsApp Flows, BVN/NIN are encrypted, and the AI assistant can draft payments but never execute them.
`Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Redis` `BullMQ` `WhatsApp Cloud API` `LangChain`

**[Crown Solar](https://github.com/Chuchom99/crown-frontend)**: e-commerce for solar equipment · [Live site](https://crowninnovativetech.com/)
A storefront and admin dashboard with a solar quote calculator: customers enter their appliances or daily usage and get a recommended system they can buy or download as a PDF.
`React` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `Node.js` `JWT`
